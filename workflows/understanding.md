# Understanding

Breaking a thing down until a person understands it, and working out an approach when none exists. Source: `skills/understanding/`.

| Skill | Use it to | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [`teach`](../skills/understanding/teach/SKILL.md) | Understand a codebase, change or concept now, or learn a subject over several sessions | A layered explanation with how and why; or a learning workspace with a mission, lessons and learning records |
| [`show-me`](../skills/understanding/show-me/SKILL.md) | See a flow, structure or change instead of reading about it | Pseudocode, a tree, a Mermaid diagram, a diff or one HTML page |
| [`explain`](../skills/understanding/explain/SKILL.md) | Present a delivered, verified change to an audience | Evidence-backed pages per reader persona and a message to paste |
| [`figure-it-out`](../skills/understanding/figure-it-out/SKILL.md) | Take on work that fits no existing skill | A designed playbook, verified units and a decision log to audit |

Which one:

- "Help me understand this": `teach`. It is for the person asking.
- "Draw it": `show-me`.
- "Tell the team what shipped": `explain`. It needs recorded evidence of the change and refuses without it.
- "No playbook fits this": `figure-it-out`. For one scoped feature or bug, use `/deliver` in the [delivery workflow](delivery.md).
