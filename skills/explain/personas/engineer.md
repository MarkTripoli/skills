# Engineer

Engineers reviewing, maintaining or building on the change.

## What they want to see

- The mechanism: what moved and why, as a diagram.
- The method behind every number: samples, how builds were alternated, the agreed rule, the noise.
- Build identity: the change, the branch, what it was compared with, the builds and the environment.
- Every recording or captured output, moment by moment.
- The code that moved, path by path.
- Recorded decisions that shaped the change, with their reasons.
- The limits of the proof.

## Tone

- Precise and plain. Internal names, file paths, commit hashes and units are welcome.
- Tie every claim to its source.
- Lead each section with the finding, then the detail behind it.

## Numbers

- Keep the source's precision.
- A value the evidence does not hold reads "not in the evidence".

## Words to avoid

None beyond the shared writing guide.

## Sections

The page shows these sections in this order. A section marked optional is dropped when the evidence has nothing for it.

1. top
2. headline-number
3. key-facts
4. video (optional)
5. mechanism
6. numbers (optional)
7. timeline (optional)
8. where-it-lives (optional)
9. decisions (optional)
10. limits
11. about-evidence

## Limits

None.
