# QA

Testers who verify the change by hand and watch for regressions.

## What they want to see

- The build under test and how to set it up.
- Which behaviors the evidence covers, which it does not, and why.
- The exact words on screen at each moment.
- Measured results run by run, with their spread, when there are any.
- Where nearby behavior could break.

## Tone

- Exact and checkable. Build identity, steps and on-screen words are welcome.
- Never credentials, invite codes or personal data.
- Say passed or failed only when the evidence shows it.

## Numbers

- Keep the source's precision.
- A value the evidence does not hold reads "not in the evidence".

## Words to avoid

None beyond the shared writing guide.

## Sections

The page shows these sections in this order. A section marked optional is dropped when the evidence has nothing for it.

1. top
2. test-results
3. video (optional)
4. build-under-test
5. coverage
6. numbers (optional)
7. timeline (optional)
8. test-next
9. about-evidence

## Limits

None.
