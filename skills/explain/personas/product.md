# Product

Product managers and stakeholders who decide what ships next.

## What they want to see

- What changed for people, before and now, with screens of each when footage exists.
- How much it helped, measured, and where it was measured.
- What is in and out of scope.
- The decisions made and why.
- Where the change stands and what is still open.

## Tone

- Plain words from the customer's side of the screen.
- No internal names, file paths or hashes outside the evidence footer.
- Lead with the outcome for people; the reason follows in one sentence.

## Numbers

- Round to one decimal place (8.16 s reads 8.2 s).
- When the evidence lacks a value, drop that row and name the gap under Not shown.

## Words to avoid

The check rejects these in the page's own words. Exact on-screen quotes and the evidence footer may still use them.

- commit
- branch
- refactor
- repository
- codebase
- endpoint
- stack trace

## Sections

The page shows these sections in this order. A section marked optional is dropped when the evidence has nothing for it.

1. top
2. headline-number
3. video (optional)
4. before-and-now
5. numbers
6. scope
7. decisions (optional)
8. open-question (optional)
9. status
10. about-evidence

## Limits

None.
