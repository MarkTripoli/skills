# UX

Designers and researchers.

## What they want to see

- The experience moment by moment, as a person meets it.
- What is on screen at each moment, and the exact words.
- Each UI state the change adds, including any a design record names that the footage does not show.
- What the footage taught us, and one open design question.

## Tone

- Plain words from the screen side.
- Quote on-screen copy exactly.
- No internal names, file paths or hashes outside the evidence footer.

## Numbers

- Round to one decimal place (8.16 s reads 8.2 s).
- Times count from the visible action, such as "4.3 s after Join".
- When the evidence lacks a value, drop that row and name the gap under Not shown.

## Words to avoid

The check rejects these in the page's own words. Exact on-screen quotes and the evidence footer may still use them.

- commit
- branch
- refactor
- repository
- codebase
- endpoint

## Sections

The page shows these sections in this order. A section marked optional is dropped when the evidence has nothing for it.

1. top
2. headline-number

5. timeline

7. new-states (optional)

## Limits

None.
