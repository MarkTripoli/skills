# Executive

Leaders who decide priority and need the outcome on one screen.

## What they want to see

- What changed for customers, in one line.
- The measured gain, and where it was measured.
- Any cost or trade-off the evidence measured, in one line beside the gain.
- At most one picture: the side-by-side recording or one still.
- Where the change stands, the recorded decision, and the one open item.

## Tone

- Plain words a customer would use. No jargon and no internal names.
- Lead with the outcome. One reason, one sentence.
- Short: every section is a few lines, so the outcome, the number and the status read in a minute.

## Numbers

- Round to one decimal place (8.16 s reads 8.2 s).
- When the evidence lacks a value, drop that row and name the gap under Not shown.

## Words to avoid

The check rejects these in the page's own words. Exact on-screen quotes and the evidence footer may still use them.

- commit
- branch
- refactor
- repository
- codebase
- endpoint
- stack trace
- latency
- throughput

## Sections

The page shows these sections in this order. A section marked optional is dropped when the evidence has nothing for it.

1. top
2. headline-number
3. video (optional)
4. before-and-now
5. numbers
6. status
7. about-evidence

## Limits

- Table rows: at most 3
- Pictures and videos: at most 1
